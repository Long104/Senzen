## 5. FAQ Honesty Audit & Line-by-Line Verdict

### Item 1: Bank connection
- **Existing text:**
  - Q: "Do I need to connect my bank?"
  - A: "No. Senzen is manual by design — you log expenses daily and track progress yourself."
- **Verdict:** `REWRITE`
- **Reason:** Good core sentiment, but needs lowercase calm register and clear framing around safety and simplicity.
- **Rewritten copy:**
  - **q:** `do i need to connect my bank?`
  - **a:** `no. senzen never asks for bank logins or credentials. you type spending into the capture bar in a few seconds.`

### Item 2: Plans definition
- **Existing text:**
  - Q: "What is a plan?"
  - A: "A plan is a budget with a duration and categories. Create one, set budgets, then track spending against it."
- **Verdict:** `REWRITE`
- **Reason:** In previous copy, plans sounded mandatory or automated. They are strictly optional budget envelopes.
- **Rewritten copy:**
  - **q:** `what is a plan?`
  - **a:** `a plan is an optional budget ceiling for a month or category. if you only want to log daily expenses without targets, you can skip plans completely.`

### Item 3: Auto-save / Nudges
- **Existing text:**
  - Q: "What does auto-save do?"
  - A: "It's a per-plan toggle. When on, Senzen nudges you to stay on track — it doesn't move money automatically."
- **Verdict:** `KILL`
- **Reason:** Violates the product truth and philosophical veto. Senzen does not send nudges, push notifications, or run fake auto-save automation.
- **Replacement Q&A:**
  - **q:** `will senzen send me notifications or reminders?`
  - **a:** `no. senzen has no push alerts, streaks, or guilt trips. it is ready when you open it and silent when you do not.`

### Item 4: Comparison to spreadsheets
- **Existing text:**
  - Q: "How is this different from a spreadsheet?"
  - A: "Plans, budgets, categories, and a daily ledger in one place — with progress bars that keep you honest."
- **Verdict:** `REWRITE`
- **Reason:** Replace vague claims with concrete utilities: fast mobile capture, search, multi-currency (THB default + 5 currencies), and one-click CSV export.
- **Rewritten copy:**
  - **q:** `how is this different from a spreadsheet?`
  - **a:** `faster entry on phone or desktop, instant day grouping, search, multi-currency support, and clean csv export whenever you want raw data.`
